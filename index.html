01The real cost of “no”

A Turned-Down Loan Shouldn’t Cost You an Entire Depository Relationship.

Every commercial loan officer knows the pain of saying “no” to an otherwise promising business client because of credit blips, tight cash flow reporting, or non-conforming collateral.

The immediate loss of loan volume hurts, but the downstream risk is far worse. When a frustrated client seeks financing elsewhere, the competing bank that approves them will demand one thing: moving all operational treasury and deposit accounts. Just like that, years of relationship building walk out the door.

Without a bridge partner

  1. You decline a solid borrower over credit, cash flow reporting, or collateral.
  2. The frustrated client takes the deal to a competing bank.
  3. The approving bank demands all treasury and deposit accounts.
  4. Years of relationship building walk out the door.

With Commercial Funding NW

  1. You make a confidential introduction, with zero obligation.
  2. We source private, non-bank capital, with conditional approval in 1–3 business days.
  3. Your client’s operating accounts stay safely at your bank.
  4. The borrower returns to your desk the moment they’re bankable.
